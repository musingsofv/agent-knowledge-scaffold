# Qualify leads against a stated customer profile

## Approach and requirements

Before adding a prospect to an outreach campaign, document how their business
and problem match the campaign's intended customer profile. Record the evidence
and mark unknowns explicitly; an available email address alone is insufficient.

## Applies when

Selecting prospects or reviewing lead quality, regardless of the CRM or outreach
tool used. This is a fictional organization's example, not a legal assessment.

## Verification

A reviewer can see the qualification evidence and unresolved questions for
each selected prospect. Delivery or reply counts alone do not establish fit.
